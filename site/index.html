Working hard, and the grades still aren’t moving?

Parents tell us this on the first call more than anything else. It’s rarely about effort. Usually one of these is getting in the way.

  • Motivation and accountability.

    Lots of students know they need to work but can’t get started, or can’t keep going. We build the habits with them and check in every week, so the effort turns into progress.

  • Exam technique.

    Knowing the content and answering the question the way the examiner wants are two different skills. We teach students how to break a question down and structure an answer for the marks.

  • Lessons they’ve switched off from.

    When lessons feel like a chore, students stop listening. Ours are live, interactive and built around real exam questions, so students stay with it and start to enjoy the subject again.

How our students study less and remember more.

Most students revise by re-reading their notes. It feels productive, and a week later most of it has gone. We teach revision techniques that research shows work better, and how examiners mark.

Active recall.

Testing yourself instead of re-reading. It’s harder, and that’s why it works.

Blurting.

Write down everything you remember about a topic, then check what’s missing. The gaps become the next lesson.

Spaced repetition.

Coming back to each topic at growing intervals, so it’s still there in May.

Think like an examiner.

We show students what examiners look for, which answers earn the marks, and what to leave out.

Questions parents ask.

What happens in the free consultation?

A short video call with the student and a parent. We talk through where the student is, agree a target grade, and you meet the tutor. There’s no obligation to go further.

Is the trial lesson free?

No. It’s a one-hour paid lesson, so you see exactly what a normal lesson is like before you commit to a plan. We’ll go through prices on the free consultation.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the level and the plan, so we talk it through on the free consultation, once we know what the student needs. There’s no charge for that call.

Why a 90-day minimum?

Real progress takes a term. Ninety days is long enough to learn the technique, practise it on exam questions and see it in the marks. Shorter than that, you’re mostly paying for catch-up.

Are lessons online?

Yes. Every lesson is live and 1-to-1 on video, so students can learn from anywhere.

How will I know it’s working?

After every lesson you get a short written summary: what was covered, how it went, and what’s next.

Which subjects do you teach?

The major GCSE and A-Level subjects, across the main exam boards. Economics is Bhavisha’s specialist subject, and we match every student to a tutor who knows their subject and board.

Who will teach my child?

Bhavisha, or a tutor she has trained personally in the Class Hero method. We make the match in step 2, the trial lesson.

Will my child definitely reach their target grade?

No honest tutor can tell you that. What we can do is build the plan, teach the technique and keep the student accountable. So far, 95% of our 112 students have reached their target grade.
