# Class Hero: the offer

> Phase 1, for Eddie's approval. Built on the Hormozi value equation and naming formula (`hormozi-business/reference/offers.md` §3, §9), the attraction → continuity model (`money-models.md` §3), and Atlas's six offer ingredients (`atlas-marketing/reference/offer/offers-that-convert.md`). Decided 2026-09-26: step 1 is a **free consultation**, and there is **no guarantee**.

## The problem it solves

A GCSE or A-Level student is working hard and it isn't showing in their grades. Usually the gap is exam technique and how they revise, not effort. Parents can see it happening but can't fix it themselves, and a tutor who just re-teaches the content doesn't fix it either.

## The outcome: the student's own target grade

The student and tutor agree a **target grade** in the consultation. Everything after that is a plan to reach it.

The site **promises the plan and the method, never the grade.** The result shows up as proof: what past students achieved, stated as fact and always attributed. That keeps the offer specific (one grade, one subject, 90 days) without reading as a guarantee.

**Offer statement**
> We help GCSE and A-Level students reach their target grade with a 90-day plan and 1-to-1 lessons from expert tutors who teach exactly how examiners mark.

**Name:** The 90-Day Target Grade Plan

**Proof line**, kept apart from the promise and never in the same sentence:
> 95% of our students have reached their target grade.
(**CONFIRM** the base: the live site says "over 100 students", the dev draft says 112.)

## Recommended hero

> **A 90-day plan to reach your target grade.**
> Live 1-to-1 GCSE and A-Level tutoring built around how examiners mark. It starts with a free consultation.
> [Book a free consultation]   95% of our students have reached their target grade

Alternatives, if Eddie prefers:

## The three steps

| | Step | When | What it is | Money |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Free consultation** | Day 1 | The student meets the tutor. We learn where they're stuck, agree a target grade and the gaps in the way, and say honestly whether we're the right fit. | Free, no obligation |
| 2 | **Trial lesson** | Week 1 | If it's a good match, we pair the student with the right tutor for a full lesson on a real topic. Both sides see how it works before committing. | Paid (price **CONFIRM**) |
| 3 | **Your 90-day plan** | Days 1-90 | A personalised plan built around the student's exam board and exam dates, then worked through lesson by lesson, with homework and feedback. Parents see progress along the way. | 90-day minimum (price and lessons per week **CONFIRM**) |

**Why 90 days, in the site's words:** "Grades move when habits change, and habits take a term to stick. Ninety days is long enough to learn the technique, practise it on real exam questions and see it in the marks."

## Language rules

- Never write: guarantee(d), promise, "you will get", "sure to", "results in X weeks".
- Results are past tense and attributed: "Our students have…", "One GCSE Maths student went from a 6 to an 8 in five weeks."
- No prices, star ratings, student counts or subject claims that aren't in CLAIMS.md as confirmed.
- CTA wording everywhere: **Book a free consultation**. On the booking page: **Pick a time**.
- British English, no em dashes, none of the writing-style skill's banned words.
