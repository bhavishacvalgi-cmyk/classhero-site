### 3. Who it's for
H2: **Working hard, and the grades still aren't moving?**
Intro: That's the most common thing parents tell us in the first call. It's rarely about effort. It usually comes down to one of these.
- **Motivation and accountability.** Lots of students know they need to work and can't get started, or can't keep going. We build the habits and check in every week so the effort turns into progress.
- **Exam technique.** Knowing the content and answering the question the way the examiner wants are two different skills. We teach students how to break a question down and structure an answer for the marks.
- **Lessons they've switched off from.** When lessons feel like a chore, students stop listening. Ours are live, interactive and tied to real exam questions, so students stay with it and start enjoying the subject again.

### 5. The method (signature: the forgetting curve)
H2: **How our students study less and remember more.**
Intro: Most students revise by re-reading their notes. It feels productive, and a week later most of it has gone. We teach three techniques that research shows work better.
- Chart caption (scroll-driven): After a lesson, memory drops away fast. Each review, spaced a little further apart, lifts it back up and slows the fall. By exam day, the topic stays put.
- **Active recall.** Testing yourself instead of re-reading. It's harder, and that's why it works.
- **Blurting.** Write down everything you remember about a topic from memory, then check what's missing. The gaps become the next lesson.
- **Spaced repetition.** Coming back to each topic at growing intervals, so it's still there in May.
- **Think like an examiner.** We show students what examiners look for, which answers earn the marks, and what to leave out. No wasted revision.
- Closing line: You deserve a social life and a full night's sleep. Studying smarter makes room for both.

### 8. FAQ
H2: **Questions parents ask.**
- **What happens in the free consultation?** A short video call (length **CONFIRM**) with the student and a parent. We talk through where the student is, agree a target grade, and you meet the tutor. There's no obligation to go further.
- **Is the trial lesson free?** No. It's a one-hour paid lesson, so you see exactly what a normal lesson is like before you commit to a plan. We'll go through prices on the free consultation.
- **How much does it cost?** It depends on the level and the plan, so we talk it through on the free consultation, once we know what the student needs. There's no charge for that call.
- **Why a 90-day minimum?** Real progress takes a term. Ninety days is long enough to learn the technique, practise it on exam questions and see it in the marks. Shorter than that, you're mostly paying for catch-up.
- **Are lessons online?** Yes. Every lesson is live, 1-to-1, on video, so students can learn from anywhere.
- **How will I know it's working?** After every lesson you get a short written summary: what was covered, how it went, and what's next.
- **Which subjects do you teach?** The major GCSE and A-Level subjects, across the main exam boards. Economics is Bhavisha's specialist subject, and we match every student to a tutor who knows their subject and board.
- **Who will teach my child?** Bhavisha or a tutor she has trained personally in the Class Hero method. We match each student in step 2.
- **Can you promise a grade?** No honest tutor can. What we can do is build the plan, teach the technique and keep the student accountable. So far, 95% of our 112 students have reached their target grade.
